guatermark benchmark

Watermark removal and quality enhancement, implemented 100% in Rust. Every number below comes from guatermark eval on 6 clean ground-truth photos (72 removal cases). The watermark, its exact pixel mask and the degraded input are generated deterministically, so the benchmark is reproducible.

33.1 dBROI PSNR after diffusion inpainting (exact mask, from 23.3 dB)
0.926ROI SSIM after removal
30.8 dBpatch synthesis with the same mask
30.08 dBenhancement PSNR (Lanczos3 baseline 29.64 dB)

Removal is mask-based, like IOPaint or Photoshop: automatic detection of arbitrary watermarks is not solved. “exact mask” is the true watermark footprint; “grown mask” adds 2 px to cover soft edges and JPEG halos, as --grow 2 would. ROI PSNR/SSIM are measured around the watermark, where the change happens.

Watermark removal

Images are stamped, saved as JPEG (q92) and then restored. fast multi-scale diffusion (recommended), patch exemplar-based synthesis. Restored images are shown in full.

Quality enhancement

The clean photo is downscaled 2x and given Gaussian noise (σ=5), then restored to the original size. The baseline is a plain Lanczos3 upscale; guatermark adds bilateral denoise and luma unsharp masking.

How to reproduce

cargo run --release -- eval

Source images: Lorem Picsum / Unsplash (see ATTRIBUTION.md). Watermarks are synthetic and applied at evaluation time. Metrics: RGB PSNR and luminance SSIM with an 11×11 Gaussian window (σ=1.5); “ROI” is the watermark bounding box, “on mark” is computed only on the watermark pixels.

guatermark · MIT · 100% Rust (no Python, no ONNX, no external models)
